# Limited Warranty Terms

_Document ID: POL-WAR-001 · Last revised: 5 February 2026 · Owner: Product Quality_

## 1. Coverage period

Every physical product we sell carries a limited warranty against defects in
materials and workmanship for **twelve months from the date of delivery**.
Electronics classified as professional-grade carry a twenty-four month period;
these are identified as such on the product page at the time of purchase.

The warranty period runs from delivery and is not extended by a repair or a
replacement issued under it.

## 2. What the warranty covers

Coverage extends to faults arising from the manufacturing process: components
that fail under normal use, finishes that separate or delaminate, seams that
part, batteries that lose the majority of their rated capacity inside the
period, and mechanisms that seize or bind.

## 3. What the warranty does not cover

- Damage from dropping, crushing, or immersion.
- Ordinary cosmetic wear including scuffs, scratches, and fading.
- Consumable parts with an expected service life shorter than the period,
  including filters, brushes, blades, and adhesive pads.
- Faults arising after repair by anyone other than an authorised service centre.
- Products bought from a reseller we have not authorised.

## 4. Making a claim

A claim requires the order reference and a description of the fault; a
photograph or short video is requested where the fault is visible. Product
Quality assesses the claim and, if upheld, arranges a repair, a replacement, or
a credit at our election.

**Warranty claims are always assessed by a human reviewer.** A support agent
may confirm whether a product is still inside its coverage period and may
explain what the warranty does and does not cover, but may not determine
whether a particular fault qualifies, and may not settle a warranty claim as an
ordinary refund. This holds regardless of the value of the item.

## 5. Relationship to the return window

The thirty-day return window and this warranty are separate entitlements. A
customer inside the return window who simply does not want the item uses the
return process. A customer with a faulty item uses this warranty, and may do so
at any point inside the coverage period, including long after the return window
has closed.

## 6. Statutory rights

Nothing here limits rights the customer holds under the consumer law of their
own jurisdiction.
