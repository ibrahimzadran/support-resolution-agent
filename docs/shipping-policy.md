# Shipping and Delivery Policy

_Document ID: POL-SHP-002 · Last revised: 28 January 2026 · Owner: Logistics_

## 1. Dispatch and delivery estimates

Orders placed before 14:00 local time are dispatched the same business day.
Every order is assigned an estimated arrival date at the time of purchase, and
that date is shown to the customer in their confirmation message and in their
account history. Estimates account for ordinary carrier variability but not for
severe weather, industrial action, or customs inspection.

## 2. Service levels

- **Standard** — five to seven business days after dispatch. No charge on
  orders above $35.
- **Expedited** — two to three business days after dispatch.
- **Overnight** — next business day when ordered before the daily cut-off.

Business days exclude weekends and public holidays observed at the dispatching
warehouse.

## 3. Consignments in progress

A consignment that has been handed to the carrier and has not yet arrived is
described as in progress. While a consignment is in progress and has not passed
its estimated arrival date, no intervention is available: we cannot reroute it,
recall it, or accelerate it, and we do not treat it as missing. The appropriate
response to a customer asking about such a consignment is to report its current
carrier status and its estimated arrival date.

## 4. Consignments presumed lost

A consignment is presumed lost once **ten days have elapsed past its estimated
arrival date** without a delivery scan. At that point the customer is entitled
to either a replacement shipment at no cost or a full credit, at the customer's
election.

Because a presumed-loss determination commits the company to a replacement or a
credit and requires a carrier trace to be opened, it is **handled by a human
reviewer**, not settled by a support agent directly, irrespective of the value
of the consignment.

Between the estimated arrival date and the tenth day afterwards, a consignment
is late but not yet presumed lost. Customers should be advised that carrier
delays of a few days are common and asked to allow the full period to elapse.

## 4a. Deliveries recorded but not received

Where the carrier has recorded a delivery but the customer states nothing
arrived, the customer should be asked to check with neighbours and around the
property, and to allow one further business day, as scans sometimes precede the
physical handover. If the item is still absent, the matter is passed to a human
reviewer to open a carrier investigation.

## 5. Address corrections

An address may be corrected without charge until the order is dispatched. Once
a consignment has been handed to the carrier the address is fixed and cannot be
altered by us.

## 6. Tracking information

A tracking reference is issued for every consignment at the point of dispatch.
Orders still awaiting dispatch have no tracking reference, and its absence does
not indicate a problem with the order.
