# Returns and Refunds Policy

_Document ID: POL-RET-004 · Last revised: 12 March 2026 · Owner: Customer Operations_

## 1. Standard return window

Merchandise purchased through our online store may be sent back for a full
refund within **30 days of the delivery date** recorded by the carrier. The
delivery date, not the order date and not the shipment date, starts the clock.
Where a carrier records no delivery scan, Customer Operations may substitute
the estimated arrival date at their discretion.

Requests received after the thirtieth day are outside the standard window.
Agents do not have discretion to extend it; a supervisor may grant a goodwill
exception, and such requests must be passed to a human reviewer.

## 2. Condition of returned merchandise

Items should be returned unused and in their original packaging. Items showing
wear beyond what is required to evaluate fit or function may be assessed a
restocking fee of up to 15%. Assessment of a restocking fee always requires
human review and may not be applied automatically.

## 3. Merchandise that cannot be returned

The following are sold on a final basis and are **not eligible for return or
refund** under any circumstance short of a manufacturing defect:

- Prepaid stored-value cards and digital gift certificates, whether delivered
  electronically or physically.
- Items sold from the clearance range, which are marked at the point of sale as
  a final transaction.
- Perishable goods, personal grooming items, and intimate apparel.
- Downloadable software once the licence key has been revealed.

A defect claim against any of the above is handled under the separate warranty
document and is not processed as a return.

## 4. Partial refunds

Where a portion of an order has already been credited back to the customer,
any further credit is limited to the **remaining uncredited balance** of that
order. The sum of all credits issued against a single order may never exceed
what the customer originally paid for it. Agents must confirm the outstanding
balance before promising an amount.

## 5. Orders already credited in full

If the full purchase price has already been returned to the customer, the order
is closed for financial purposes. No further credit may be issued. Customers
who believe they were credited the wrong amount should be passed to a human
reviewer rather than issued a second credit.

## 6. Frequent returners

Accounts that have received **more than three** credits in the preceding twelve
months are flagged for manual review. Agents may not settle a further claim on
such an account on their own authority, regardless of how small the amount is
or how clearly the claim otherwise qualifies. This is an anti-abuse control and
it takes precedence over ordinary eligibility.

## 7. Cancelled orders

An order cancelled before dispatch was never charged to completion and
therefore has nothing to credit back. Customers asking about money owed on a
cancelled order should be told that no charge was captured; if they can show a
charge on their statement, the matter goes to a human reviewer as a possible
billing error.

## 8. Timing of credits

Once approved, funds are returned to the original payment method. Card issuers
typically post the credit within five to seven business days; some banks take
up to two full statement cycles. We cannot accelerate a bank's posting time.
